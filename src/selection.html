    Interstellar

    When Earth faces environmental collapse, a group of astronauts undertakes a dangerous mission through a newly discovered wormhole. They must search across distant galaxies to find a new habitable home for humanity before time runs out. Along the way, they confront the limits of science, human resilience, and love.

  • Action Sci-Fi Thriller

    Inception

    A skilled thief who steals valuable corporate secrets from deep within the subconscious during the dream state is offered a chance at redemption. To clear his name, he must perform the near-impossible task of planting an idea into a target's mind instead of stealing one. As the boundaries between dreams and reality blur, dangerous subconscious threats emerge to stop his team.

  • Crime Drama Mystery

    The Godfather

    The aging patriarch of an organized crime dynasty attempts to transfer control of his clandestine empire to his reluctant youngest son. As rival families threaten their business and family safety, the reluctant heir is pulled deeper into a violent underworld. It is a powerful saga of loyalty, betrayal, and the corrupting nature of power.

  • Action Sci-Fi Superhero

    The Dark Knight

    With the help of trusted allies, Batman maintains a fragile order in Gotham City until a criminal mastermind known as the Joker unleashes chaos. The villain tests the limits of justice and forces the vigilante to question the line between hero and villain. As the city descends into anarchy, difficult sacrifices must be made to save its moral core.

  • Drama Romance Classic

    Titanic

    A young aristocratic woman falling under the expectations of an arranged marriage meets a free-spirited artist aboard the ill-fated luxury liner. Despite their vast class differences, they form a deep bond during the ship's maiden voyage across the Atlantic. Their romance turns into a desperate struggle for survival when the ship collides with a massive iceberg.

  • Sci-Fi Action Classic

    The Matrix

    A computer hacker leads a mundane life until a mysterious woman leads him to an underground rebellion against intelligent machines. He uncovers the shocking truth that his everyday reality is an elaborate simulated illusion designed to control humanity. Armed with new abilities, he joins the fight to liberate the human race from digital bondage.

  • Biopic Historical Drama Drama

    Schindler's List

    In German-occupied Poland during World War II, a charismatic businessman arrives looking to profit from the war effort by opening a factory. As he witnesses the horrific persecution of the Jewish population by the regime, his motivations gradually turn from profit to humanity. Using his fortune and influence, he risks everything to protect hundreds of innocent lives.

  • Adventure Fantasy Action

    The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring

    A modest hobbit inherits a powerful ring that holds the fate of his entire world in its balance. Joined by a diverse group of heroes, he sets out on a perilous journey to destroy the artifact in the fires where it was forged. Evil forces pursue them relentlessly across vast regions as their alliance is tested at every step.

  • Biopic Historical Drama Science

    The Imitation Game

    During the darkest days of World War II, brilliant mathematician Alan Turing is recruited by British intelligence to crack an unbreakable enemy communication code. Working alongside a secretive team, he races against time to design a revolutionary calculating machine. His groundbreaking efforts help turn the tide of the war while he guards a deeply personal secret.

  • Drama Psychological Mystery

    Whiplash

    An ambitious young jazz drummer enrolls at an elite music conservatory with dreams of achieving greatness. He catches the attention of a notoriously ruthless instructor whose teaching methods rely on fear, intimidation, and mental pressure. As the student pushes himself to physical and emotional extremes, his pursuit of perfection threatens to consume his life.

  • Psychological Mystery Thriller

    Shutter Island

    A U.S. Marshal and his new partner travel to a remote island asylum for the criminally insane to investigate the sudden disappearance of a patient. As a powerful hurricane cuts off communication with the mainland, strange clues and unhelpful staff make the investigation increasingly dangerous. The officer must unravel the hospital's hidden secrets while confronting disturbing memories of his own past.

  • Adventure Drama Space

    The Martian

    During a fierce storm on Mars, an astronaut is presumed dead and accidentally left behind by his crew. Finding himself stranded on a hostile planet with limited supplies, he relies on his ingenuity, scientific knowledge, and spirit to survive. Meanwhile, scientists back on Earth work tirelessly to plan a daring rescue mission against astronomical odds.

  • Sci-Fi Thriller Cyberpunk

    Blade Runner 2049

    Thirty years after the events of the original story, a new law officer unearths a long-buried secret that could plunge what remains of society into chaos. His discovery leads him on a quest to track down a former blade runner who has been missing for decades. Together, they must navigate a dystopian landscape to preserve the delicate truth.

  • Historical Drama Drama War

    1917

    At the height of the First World War, two young British soldiers are assigned a seemingly impossible mission behind enemy lines. They must cross dangerous territory to deliver an urgent message that could stop a deadly ambush and save hundreds of lives. Racing against the clock, every step brings them face-to-face with the grim realities of the front line.

  • Action Thriller Crime

    Heat

    A methodical master thief leads an elite crew executing high-stakes heists across Los Angeles. A obsessed police detective becomes determined to bring the crew down, sparking a high-stakes game of cat and mouse across the city. As both men struggle to balance their personal lives with their fixations, their inevitable clash tests their mutual respect and survival skills.

  • Biopic Historical Drama Science

    Oppenheimer

    During World War II, theoretical physicist J. Robert Oppenheimer is appointed to lead the top-secret Manhattan Project to develop the first nuclear weapons. As scientists race against time to end the war, Oppenheimer must grapple with the catastrophic power and moral weight of their creation. The aftermath exposes him to intense political scrutiny and personal fallout.

  • Media Satire Psychological Drama Reality v Illusion

    The Truman Show

    An insurance salesman lives a seemingly peaceful life in an idyllic island town, unaware that his entire world is a massive television set. Every person around him is an actor, and his daily routine is broadcast live to millions of viewers around the globe. As strange occurrences reveal cracks in the illusion, he sets out to uncover the truth and escape his controlled reality.
